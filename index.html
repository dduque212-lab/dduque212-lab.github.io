Project 01

4-Storey Classroom Building

Roof deck · ₱80M budget · ETABS, cross-checked with STAAD.Pro

Approach

I was tasked with the structural modeling and verification of a 4-storey classroom building with a roof deck, a project budgeted at ₱80M. Using ETABS as the primary software, I cross-referenced the model with STAAD.Pro to eliminate design anomalies and ensure maximum structural reliability. Through this dual-software approach, I evaluated critical seismic vulnerabilities, including soft-storey, mass and torsional irregularities, as well as P-delta effects. The workflow gave a code-compliant, optimized design tailored to the project’s budget and safety requirements.

Tacloban City, Leyte · ₱150M budget · 33 m × 71 m footprint

A · Hip truss roof system

Hip Truss, Third-Floor Sports Court

Designed the critical hip truss system, approx. 30 m × 45 m, analyzed in STAAD.Pro for an extreme design wind speed of 290 km/h. Double angle bars deliver a cost-efficient yet robust framework against catastrophic typhoon loads.

  • STAAD.Pro
  • 290 km/h
  • Double angles

B · Mat foundation

Mat Foundation

Extracted superstructure reactions from a specialized MIDAS model and exported them to SAFE to design a heavy-duty mat. Validated against uplift, punching shear, sliding, overturning and soil settlement under all load combinations.

  • MIDAS
  • SAFE
  • Code checks

C · Front canopy

Front Canopy, 8.94 m High

Two-column architectural canopy with substantial eccentricities. Modeled in ETABS, reactions exported to SAFE. Engineered an isolated footing with strap beam to resist severe uplift and overturning.
